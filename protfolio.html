LLM vs. market-data sentiment scoring

Research project · with Prof. Te-Wei Wang, UIS Department of MIS

In progress · for publication
The goal

Test whether ChatGPT and Claude score financial news sentiment as reliably as Alpha Vantage, using Netflix and Walmart news.

Pipeline
Alpha Vantage news→NFLX · WMT→ChatGPT + Claude scores→compare
Scoring scale
−1 bearish+1 bullish
What it does
  • Uses Alpha Vantage's overall sentiment, ticker sentiment and relevance scores as the baseline.
  • Has ChatGPT and Claude score the same news summaries, overall and per ticker (aspect-based).
  • Re-runs the scoring twice a week to test whether each model gives consistent answers over time.
